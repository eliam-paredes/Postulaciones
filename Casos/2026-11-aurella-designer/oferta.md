## Texto literal de la oferta

Aurella House is a Shopify migration and growth partner for established ecommerce brands. Our clients have real revenue and loyal customers, and they've been running for years. But their website, systems and brand haven't kept up with how good the business really is.

We're small, senior and growing. Right now the founder handles strategy, creative direction and client relationships, and our developer builds on Shopify. This role takes ownership of design: from a client's strategy document to their brand and website. Every client starts with a Blueprint, a deep strategy session and a 12-month plan. Then we renovate the business (usually a Shopify migration and rebuild) and stay on as their growth partner.

We care about craft, and about how our clients feel throughout the process. Our clients are established, time-poor founders. They need work that looks elevated, explains itself clearly and moves their business forward.

© Get on Board.
Job functions

You'll be responsible for the design side of our client work and our own brand, working directly with the founder.

    Designing Shopify homepages, product pages, collection pages and landing pages in Figma, ready for development
    Turning strategy into beautiful documents: Blueprint strategy decks, client presentations and proposals
    Brand work for Aurella House and our clients: layouts, case studies, social assets and visual systems
    Designing with Shopify's sections and blocks in mind, so our developer can build it cleanly and clients can edit it themselves
    Working closely with our developer on handoff, responsive behaviour and QA
    Bringing your own ideas (layout, motion, imagery) rather than waiting to be told
    Flagging gaps or ambiguities in a brief early rather than guessing

Success looks like: designs are approved with few revision rounds, client documents look like they came from a premium firm, and the founder can step back from hands-on design.
Qualifications and requirements

    2+ years of design experience, with a portfolio covering both ecommerce/web and presentation or brand work
    Strong in Figma: auto layout, components and organised files a developer can work from
    An editorial, elevated eye: considered typography, white space and restraint
    Understanding of how ecommerce sites work: product pages, navigation, conversion basics
    Able to take a strategy or messy notes and turn them into a clear visual story
    Strong written English and proactive, async communication
    Independent: this role exists so the founder can creative direct, not manage design day to day

Desirable skills

    Experience designing for Shopify themes specifically
    Brand identity work for product businesses
    Basic motion or animation for web
    Experience designing strategy decks, reports or pitch documents
    Spanish and strong English speaker

Conditions

    Approximately 20–40 hours per month, around 5–10 hours per week to start. Some weeks will be heavier depending on project phase.
    Fully remote, flexible hours. We care about output and communication, not when you're online.
    Ongoing contract with room to grow as we take on more clients
    Paid in USD ($25–30) per hour, depending on experience)
